Definition 4.2 Regular-disk, broken Lefschetz, and purely wrinkled fibrations
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 4.1

Lean declarations

  • S4TuringPoisson.RegularDiskFibration
  • S4TuringPoisson.BrokenLefschetzFibration
  • S4TuringPoisson.PurelyWrinkledFibration

A RegularDiskFibration is a smooth map \(f\colon M\to N\) with a disk-shaped open region of regular values over which \(f\) is smoothly trivial with compact surface fiber (imposing no critical-locus normal form). BrokenLefschetzFibration extends it by requiring every critical point on \(M\) to be a real Lefschetz point or an indefinite fold; PurelyWrinkledFibration requires every critical point on \(M\) to be an indefinite fold or a wrinkled cusp.

Theorem 4.8 Lekili local moves and compactly supported broken-to-wrinkled homotopy (Track A)

Explicit smooth \(1\)-parameter families \(\mathbb {R}^4\to \mathbb {R}^2\) model Lekili’s Moves 1–4 with closed-form indefinite-fold and wrinkled-cusp charts (including the swallowtail flip Move 4). Shrinking the regular base disk and gluing compactly supported Move 1 bumps deforms every BrokenLefschetzFibration \(B\) on a compact Hausdorff \(4\)-manifold via a smooth global homotopy \(H\colon \mathbb {R}\times M\to \mathrm{Base}\) supported in a compact neighborhood \(K\) of the real Lefschetz points (disjoint from all regular and fold points) to a PurelyWrinkledFibration \(P\).

Proof ▶

Classify cusps by Weierstrass preparation (\(d=3\)) and indefinite folds by implicit chart inversion (\(d=2\)), prove finite isolation of real Lefschetz critical points by compactness, shrink baseRegion to half radius, and eliminate Lefschetz points via pairwise-disjoint compactly supported homotopies \(t\mapsto \mathrm{compactLefschetzWrinkle}(t s_0)\).

There is an explicit global BrokenLefschetzFibration and PurelyWrinkledFibration \(f_{\mathrm{ADK}}\colon S^4\to S^2\) realizing the round-handle decomposition of Auroux–Donaldson–Katzarkov, with critical set equal to the equatorial circle \(\{ x_2=x_3=x_4=0\} \) of indefinite quadratic folds, embedded \(C^\infty \) regular fibers diffeomorphic to \(S^2\) (north) and \(T^2=S^1\times S^1\) (south), and explicit smooth bundle trivializations over both hemispheres.

Theorem 4.10 Local regular-disk fibration and Track B separation on closed \(4\)-manifolds
✓
#

Uses

  • S4TuringPoisson.not_isGenericallyRankTwo_closedManifoldLocal

Every nonempty compact Hausdorff smooth \(4\)-manifold \(M\) equipped with a positive volume \(4\)-form \(V_M\) admits a positively co-oriented RegularDiskFibration \(M\to S^2\) with fiber \(S^2\) (ofPositiveVolumeForm, Track B), constructed locally inside an orientation-preserving coordinate ball and constant outside it (hence proved not generically rank \(2\) and unable to inhabit AdmitsWrinkledOrBrokenLefschetzFibration).

Proof ▶

Compose an orientation-preserving coordinate-ball chart into \(M\) with a radial cut-off blend of s4Fibration collapsing the exterior to a single point outside s4BaseRegion; compactness of \(M\) implies the exterior has nonempty interior inside \(\mathrm{Crit}(f)\).
