Theorem 8.8 Leafwise lift equals a Poisson-Hamiltonian flow

  • S4TuringPoisson.composePoissonHamiltonianTimeOneFlows_map_liftPlanarHamiltonian_eq

For an autonomous planar Hamiltonian \(P\), the time-one flow of \(X_{\tilde P}\) equals the leafwise lift of \(P\)’s disk isotopy; the same holds for finite lists.

Proof ▶

\(\iota _V\Omega = d\tilde P\wedge f^*\mu \) because \(f^*(d\rho )\wedge f^*\mu =0\) on a surface base; then ODE uniqueness on the time-rescaled trajectory \(u\mapsto P_{u\rho (b)}(y)\).
