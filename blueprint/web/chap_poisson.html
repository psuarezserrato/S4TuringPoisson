A smooth skew anchor \(\# _\pi \colon T^*M\to TM\); a Poisson structure is one whose bracket satisfies the Jacobi identity. \(X_h=-\# _\pi (dh)\), Poisson maps preserve brackets, and \(\mathrm{rank}_x\pi \) is the dimension of the image of \(\# _{\pi ,x}\).

For \(f\colon M^4\to N^2\), a volume form \(\Omega \) and an area form \(\mu _N\), the bracket defined by \(\{ g,h\} \, \Omega = dg\wedge dh\wedge f^*\mu _N\); \(\pi \) is generically rank \(2\) when \(\{ x\in M\mid \mathrm{rank}_x\pi =2\} \) is open and dense in \(M\).
