1 Status and conventions

This blueprint describes the Lean 4 formalization of P. Suárez-Serrato, Hamiltonian and Turing complete flows on smooth \(4\)-manifolds (S4TCdiffeo-Aug-2026.tex), together with the smoothing theorem of Cardona–Miranda–Peralta-Salas–Presas (CMPP21, Thm. 5.2) and the Auroux–Donaldson–Katzarkov (ADK05) and Lekili (Lek09) wrinkled/broken Lefschetz fibration machinery on which it depends. All nodes carrying \leanok are proved in Lean (toolchain v4.36.0, Mathlib and TauCeti v4.36.0) and depend only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

The main results on \(S^4\) (??) carry no hypotheses and cover both the explicit definite-fold projection model and the global ADK broken Lefschetz fibration on \(S^4\) (theorem 4.9) together with Lekili’s local \(\mathbb {R}^4\to \mathbb {R}^2\) normal-form moves and compactly supported broken-to-purely-wrinkled homotopy theorem (theorem 4.8), preserving both the aligned volume form and (via Moser conjugation, theorem 5.4) the exact round volume form \(\Omega _{S^4}\). The class HasCMPPCantorCirculationHamiltonians is globally inhabited (theorem 7.5), and every target open set in TuringCompleteWitness is certified by a computable enumerator of rational open rectangles (definition 2.2).
